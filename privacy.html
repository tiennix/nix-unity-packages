Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Our packages

NIXDeveloper packages (including NIX Audio) run inside your Unity project only. They make no network requests, contain no analytics or tracking, and collect no personal data — in the Editor or in your builds.

Local settings

  • NIX Audio saves BGM / SFX volume and mute in Unity's PlayerPrefs on the player's device.
  • Editor tools save window preferences in Unity's EditorPrefs and backups of edited audio files inside your project.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those services may log standard request data under their own privacy policies. The site itself sets no cookies.
